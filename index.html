More about CalibratedNotes +

Built from the ground up around real pen input — pressure-aware strokes, a lasso and eraser that keep up with your hand, and per-page storage that stays fast even on notebooks with hundreds of pages. Diagrams and handwriting live side by side without getting in each other's way.

Workshop notes

What is SextonsWorkshop? +

SextonsWorkshop is Aiden Sexton's independent studio — one person designing, building, and shipping precise things. The first release is CalibratedNotes, a handwriting and note-taking app for iPad built around Apple Pencil, live now on the App Store. Alongside it, the Light-Up Ball is in development as the workshop's first physical object.

Where is the Light-Up Ball made? +

It's currently being designed and prototyped in-house. Manufacturing details, materials, and where the first run will be produced will be shared here as those decisions are finalized.

How do I get support for CalibratedNotes? +

Visit the support site for troubleshooting, feature requests, and the student discount program. If you don't find what you need there, you can reach the workshop directly by email.

What's next on the bench? +

Continued refinement of CalibratedNotes based on real usage, alongside the first physical prototypes of the Light-Up Ball. Both will get updates posted here as they're ready.

Get in touch +

For support, press, or anything else — reach the workshop at sextonsworkshop@outlook.com.
